Tips & tricks

Concrete, low-effort ways to shrink your footprint. Add the ones that fit into your tracker.

Home energy

Unplug idle chargers

Chargers and electronics draw power even when switched off. A power strip you can flip off at night stops the drain.

Home energy

Switch to LED bulbs

LEDs use a fraction of the energy of incandescent bulbs and last far longer, so it pays for itself over time.

Water

Fix the drip

A single leaky faucet can waste thousands of gallons a year. A new washer is usually a five-minute fix.

Water

Shorter showers

Trimming a couple of minutes off your shower adds up fast, especially with a low-flow showerhead.

Transport

Combine errands

Batching trips into one loop instead of several short drives cuts both fuel use and cold-engine emissions.

Transport

Try one car-free day

Pick a regular day to walk, bike, or take transit instead of driving — it only has to be one day to start a habit.

Waste

Learn your local recycling rules

Rules vary a lot by city. A few minutes checking your local guidelines prevents whole bins being rejected.

Waste

Start small with composting

A countertop bin for fruit and vegetable scraps is enough to start diverting food waste from the landfill.

Food

One plant-based meal a week

You don't have to go all-in — swapping even one meal a week for a plant-based one adds up over a year.

Food

Plan meals to cut food waste

A rough plan before you shop means less food bought on impulse and forgotten in the back of the fridge.
