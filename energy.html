Home & Energy

Energy audit of your home.

Doing an energy audit of your home can allow you to fully understand what you are actually using and what you can cut. It will save you money on your electricity bill and help fight climate change all at once! The energy auditor will be able to determine whether you have the highest efficiency and energy-saving devices for your home.

Find new ways to light your home.

Many people have switched to compact fluorescent bulbs (CFLs) in recent years, but you can further reducte your electricity usage (which will reduce your electricity costs and reduce emissions) by using LEDs. Many of these bulbs use a quarter of the energy CFLs use.

use your thermostat correctly.

This is a classic, but some people forget to keep their thermostat lower in the winter and higher in the summer to avoid massive heating/cooling loads. A lot of extra energy can be saved by decreasing the gap of the temperature outside and what you have set on your thermostat.

Wash clothes with cold water.

When you can, you should wash your clothes with colder water since around 75% to 90% of the electricity a washing machince consumes is from heating water.

Change your energy source.

You can ask your utility copmany if your home's energy comes from oil, coal or gas. If allowed, you should switch to wind or solar power, or even install solar panels on your roof to generate energy for your home. These renewable sources of energy are amazing for reducing your carbon footprint. And by switching, you can reduce your carbon footprint by up to 1.5 tons of CO2e per year.
