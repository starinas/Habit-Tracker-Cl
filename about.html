Who are we?

Hello! We are Anya and Peter, two undergraduate math majors at Villanova University passionate about the environment and finding ways to help reduce climate change.

Our Goal

We built EnviroTracker to spread awareness on both company and individual impacts on the environment and climate change as a whole. We want to provide a habit tracker so that you, as an individual, can acquire better habits for yourself which will impact those around you, local businesses, and the environment. It’s a win-win-win! And by learning about big U.S. companies and corporations and their impact on climate change, we hope to convince you to support local businesses and environmental-friendly legislation.

We believe that it is very important to understand how certain companies affect climate change. How the factories they are using pollute the air and how the way they waste materials (especially those made of plastic) can pollute certain environments like watersheds or forests. Although the government has some policies put in place to help limit these effects, it is never enough. We need your help to spread awareness and raise our voices in order to be seen by the government and have our voices heard.

What you should know

No Log-In System

You are able to log your everyday habits without having to create an account! Your data is stored within your browser so clearing your browser data will clear your habit tracker.

Built for streaks

Habits stick through repetition. The tracker is built around daily logging and visible streaks, not one-off goals.

Interactive

You are able to create your own habits to keep track of what you do everyday. This way you can personalize your experience with the tracker.
