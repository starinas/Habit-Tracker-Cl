Why this tracker exists

Big environmental problems get solved by lots of small, repeated choices. This is a low-friction place to keep track of yours.

Simple by design

No accounts, no sign-up. Your log lives in this browser so you can start tracking in seconds.

Built for streaks

Habits stick through repetition. The tracker is built around daily logging and visible streaks, not one-off goals.

Personal, not preachy

You choose the habits that fit your life. Add or remove any of them at any time from the tracker page.

A note on data

Everything you log is stored locally in your browser's storage and isn't sent anywhere. Clearing your browser data will clear your log too.
