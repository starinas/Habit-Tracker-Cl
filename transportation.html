Transportation

Electric vehicles.

Consider an electric vehicle when shopping. These cars help reduce air pollution as well as gas emissions compared to gas or diesel-powered vehicles. By switching, you could reduce your carbon footprint by up to 2 tons of CO2e per year. Even a hybrid vehicle can save you up to 700 kilograms of CO2e per year.

Public Transportation.

Using public transportation can reduce carbon emission by 30 to 45 percent. This means there are less cars on the road which leads to both less gas emissions and less traffic on the roads. This helps reduce your carbon footprint while allowing you to connect with those in your community!

Bike or walk when you can.

Biking/walking to your destination allows for less cars on the road (reduces carbon emissions) and is also good for your health. So both you and the environment benefit from biking or walking.

Carpooling.

Carpooling turns 2+ vehicles on the road into 1! Consider carpooling whenever possible to help reduce your carbon footprint and burn less gas. This will also help foster community and strengthen your relationships.

Alternatives to flying.

Whenever possible, try not to fly. An estimated 850 million tons of CO2 emissions are attributable to air travel every year. If you must fly, try your best to make the trip in one go (no layovers) since landings and takeoffs use a lot of fuel. You can also consider buying carbon offsets for your flights.
