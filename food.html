Reduce Food Waste

Composting!

Instead of throwing out certain foods, turn them into compost or find a composting center near you! Starting your own garden is a perfect way to directly utilize composting. This allows food to not rot in a landfill and instead help produce more food!

Buy organic and local products.

When buying locally sourced goods, you are helping the economy and reducing carbon footprint since the goods didn't have to be transported or driven far. And organic foods use less chemicals for growth which means there will be less runoff into rivers, lakes and oceans.

Grow and eat more vegetables.

Eating more vegetables and less meat/dairy can significantly lower your environmental impact. This is because producing plant-based foods genreal result in fewer greenhouse gas emissions and require less energy, land and water.

Throw away less food.>

When you throw food away, not only are you wasting the food but you're also wasting the resources and energy that were used to grow, produce, package, and transport it. When food rots in a landfill, it produces methane, a powerful greenhouse gas.

Plant native species.

If you have a garden or even any plants near or outside your home, check to see if they are native species. There are apps online to help. Try to replace non-native with native species. This will help other plants, animals and insects in the area and improve biodiversity.
