Reduce Waste

Reduce, reuse, repair and recycle.

Many things we use in our day-to-day life, such as electronics, clothes and things containing plastic, cause carbon emissions every step of the way. From the extraction of materials to manufacturing and transporting goods. Buying less new clothes and materials helps reduce gas emissions and recycling our materials reduces water pollutants and saves energy since fewer new products would be necessary. Buying used products helps people waste less and reduces your carbon footprint.

Use reusable bags.

Americans throw away around 100 billion plastic bags every year. These bags take about 300 years to degrade and spew tiny toxic particles into our soils and water while they are breaking down. These particles make their way into our food and water and affect both us and animals. By buying reusable bags and refusing the store’s plastic (or even paper) bags will significantly reduce your carbon footprint.

Throw away waste properly.

Some waste is impossible to recycle, such as styrofoam, ceramics and certain sanitary products. It is important that these materials are disposed of properly so they don’t find their ways into our living environment or bodies of water. Participating in stream clean-ups and spreading the word of how to recycle/throw away materials properly will help everyone.

Be smart with your money.

Every single thing you buy has an effect on the planet. Buy from companies who are committed to cutting their gas emissions and reducing waste. You have authority over where you buy items from and what types of things you buy, be sure to check where you are buying from to reduce your carbon footprint.
